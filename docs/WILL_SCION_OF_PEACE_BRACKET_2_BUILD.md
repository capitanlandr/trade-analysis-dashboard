# Will, Scion of Peace — Bracket 2 build

## Build thesis

This is a top-down Will deck. The central turn is: gain life during the main phase, activate Will, and convert the temporary generic-mana discount into a large white or blue spell. Those spells draw cards, make a board, remove permanents, or present a visible alternate win. The list deliberately avoids deterministic combo lines and current Commander Bracket 2 Game Changers.

Will's Oracle text is: “Vigilance. {T}: Spells you cast this turn that are white and/or blue cost {X} less to cast, where X is the amount of life you gained this turn. Activate only as a sorcery.” The discount reduces generic mana only, so the deck keeps a high count of white and blue sources and does not treat Will as unconditional free mana.

## Role counts

Counts below are primary role counts; cards can serve more than one role.

| Role | Count | What it does |
|---|---:|---|
| Lifegain / Will enablers | 15 | Turns on Will, supplies life for payoffs, or rewards the lifegain plan. |
| Ramp | 10 | Gets Will and the large spells online earlier. |
| Card draw / selection | 14 | Finds lifegain, lands, interaction, and the next large spell. |
| Payoffs | 10 | Converts the discount into tokens, alternate wins, or a winning board. |
| Enhancers | 4 | Makes lifegain or the resulting combat damage matter more. |
| Removal / answers | 11 | Answers creatures, artifacts, enchantments, and opposing haymakers. |
| Lands | 38 | Includes three MDFCs; 18 basics keep the mana stable. |

## Card-by-card rationale

### Commander and lifegain engine

- **Will, Scion of Peace** — The engine; vigilance lets it attack while still activating, but the sorcery restriction requires main-phase lifegain.
- **Soul Warden / Soul's Attendant / Lunarch Veteran** — Repeatable one-mana life triggers that turn ordinary creature development into Will fuel.
- **Authority of the Consuls** — Gains life against creature decks and slows haste attacks.
- **Nyx-Fleece Ram** — Reliable upkeep lifegain that also blocks small attackers.
- **Angel of Vitality** — Scales life gain and becomes a meaningful evasive body once life is high.
- **Rhox Faithmender / Boon Reflection** — Enhancers that make every later life trigger a larger Will discount.
- **Chaplain's Blessing** — A deliberately modest one-mana burst trigger; its job is to make a turn-two/turn-three Will activation possible.
- **Revitalize / Union of the Third Path** — Instant-speed life gain plus cards; Union is especially good when the hand is full and life is low.
- **Congregate / Sanguine Sacrament** — Large life bursts that can make a single Will activation pay for a major spell.
- **Celestine, the Living Saint** — Converts lifegain into a body and returns a useful permanent from the graveyard.
- **Light of Hope** — Flexible lifegain, counter placement, or enchantment interaction; it is not included as a pure one-life effect.

### Ramp

- **Sol Ring** — The strongest early acceleration, used here to reach Will or a large spell ahead of schedule.
- **Arcane Signet** — Reliable two-color fixing that keeps the discounted spells’ colored requirements covered.
- **Azorius Signet / Talisman of Progress** — Two-mana fixing that accelerates while producing both colors.
- **Mind Stone** — Early ramp that remains useful as a card later.
- **Thought Vessel** — Ramp plus no maximum hand size for the large draw turns.
- **Wayfarer's Bauble** — Converts two mana into a basic land and improves the deck’s colored-source count.
- **Marble Diamond / Sky Diamond** — Slower two-mana rocks that are acceptable at this deliberately moderate power level.
- **Knight of the White Orchid** — A creature-based catch-up ramp spell that can attack or carry True Conviction later.

### Draw and selection

- **Ponder / Preordain / Brainstorm** — Cheap selection that finds lands, lifegain, or the next payoff.
- **Frantic Search** — Filters while untapping lands, making it unusually useful during a Will turn.
- **Fact or Fiction** — Instant-speed card advantage that can be held alongside interaction.
- **Behold the Multiverse** — Two cards now or later, with foretell smoothing the early turns.
- **Mulldrifter** — A card-draw creature that can also become a body when mana is plentiful.
- **Kwain, Itinerant Meddler** — Repeated table draw with a life-gain rider that helps activate Will.
- **Drogskol Reaver** — Turns each life-gain event into a card and is a substantial lifelink threat.
- **Sphinx's Revelation** — The ideal Will payoff: cards and life scale together.
- **Finale of Revelation** — A large refill that can untap lands and reset the hand at high X.
- **Blue Sun's Zenith** — Flexible instant-speed draw that shuffles back for later use.
- **Silver Scrutiny** — Scalable draw that can be cast at instant speed when X is large enough.
- **Stroke of Genius** — A second flexible X-draw effect that can target an opponent in the late game.

### Payoffs and enhancers

- **Storm Herd** — A dramatic, life-total-scaled token payoff that rewards stabilizing first.
- **Finale of Glory** — Converts a large discount into a wide army, with extra Angels at high X.
- **White Sun's Zenith** — Instant-speed Cats provide a flexible end-step or combat payoff.
- **Secure the Wastes** — The low-end token spell that can also become a major Will turn.
- **White Sun's Twilight** — Makes Phyrexians, gains life, and can clear small opposing creatures.
- **Martial Coup** — Board reset plus a token army when the deck has accumulated mana.
- **Approach of the Second Sun** — A visible, telegraphed alternate win that asks the deck to draw through seven cards.
- **Beacon of Immortality** — A large life burst that both fuels Will and creates a huge follow-up turn.
- **Test of Endurance** — A slow alternate win that opponents can see and answer.
- **Felidar Sovereign** — Another telegraphed life-total payoff that is a creature rather than an enchantment.
- **True Conviction** — The enhancer: lifelink stabilizes the deck and double strike lets token armies close games.

### Answers and removal

- **Swords to Plowshares** — Efficient creature removal with a small lifegain benefit to the opponent.
- **Path to Exile** — Efficient creature exile for threats that must not reach the graveyard.
- **Generous Gift** — Answers any permanent at the cost of giving a modest blocker.
- **Stroke of Midnight** — Flexible instant-speed permanent removal.
- **Heliod's Intervention** — Scales from targeted artifact/enchantment removal into a large lifegain spell.
- **Fumigate** — Creature reset that replenishes the life total for a follow-up Will activation.
- **Austere Command** — A flexible sweeper whose modes can preserve the deck’s token plan.
- **Counterspell** — Broad protection for Will, a payoff, or a key permanent.
- **Negate** — Low-cost coverage for opposing noncreature haymakers.
- **Dovin's Veto** — Uncounterable protection against noncreature spells.
- **Seal Away** — Cheap flash interaction for an attacking or tapped creature.

## Likely cuts and upgrade paths

The first cuts if the deck underperforms are Chaplain's Blessing, Marble Diamond, Sky Diamond, Seal Away, and Felidar Sovereign. They are serviceable at Bracket 2 but have the lowest floor or are the least flexible cards in their slots. If the table is stronger, upgrade those slots toward more efficient interaction, better draw, and stronger land quality; if the table is slower, retain them and add more expensive X-spell payoffs. Avoid adding fast-mana Game Changers or deterministic Will untap loops if the goal remains Bracket 2.

## Test plan

Goldfish ten opening hands plus the first nine cards seen. Record whether the deck has a proactive two-mana play, a colored source for Will, a lifegain trigger before activation, and a payoff. The first tuning question is not whether a single payoff is powerful; it is whether the deck reliably assembles “land, early development, life gain, Will activation, discounted spell” without needing a tutor.
